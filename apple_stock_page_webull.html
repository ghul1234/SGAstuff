SGA Network Trading Guidelines

The SGA Network facilitates direct multi-currency trading of equities without requiring USD as a manual intermediary clearing channel.

Atomic T+0 Settlement: Trades settle instantly against corresponding localized liquidity reserves, bypassing delayed wire clearings.

Liquidity Rules: Swapping between sovereign currencies incurs a standard transaction charge of 10 basis points (0.10%). A flat $0.50 settlement fee covers ledger clearance.

By selecting a payment currency in the dropdown, the interface dynamically quotes and routes the trade directly via optimal tranches.
